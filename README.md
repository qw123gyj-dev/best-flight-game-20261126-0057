SKYHAWK — Three Valleys: a chase-view flight game. Take off from the valley strip, thread the rings through Green Valley, fight the Sky Pirate Galleon in Canyon Pass, ride the storm past the Tempest Queen, and bring her home onto the Skyhaven runway. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best flight game" bounty, $500 in USDC.

Three zones, two bosses, twelve power-ups: fuel cells, repair kits, shields, turbo boost, twin and spread cannons, homing missiles, magnets, double score, extra airframes, flares and an autopilot assist. Fuel burns with throttle, damage turns a sortie into decisions, and the landing has to be earned — line up, slow down, put her on the runway.

Controls: arrows steer and climb, A/D steer, W/S throttle, Shift afterburner, Space or click to fire, P pause, M mute. The mouse steers too. The HUD shows speed, altitude, heading, an attitude indicator, throttle, fuel and hull — plus a compass, a boss health bar and a reticle.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the game is plain static files under `game/`, no build step, no network request.
